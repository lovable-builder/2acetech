# Legal audit of 2acetech.pl, November 2026

Date of audit: 20 November 2026. Seller: 2ACE sp. z o.o. (KRS 0001267111, NIP 1133212948). Scope: Polish and EU law applicable to an online shop selling physical goods to consumers in Poland by dropshipping.

**Important note.** This is a draft audit based on public legal sources, on the code in this repository and on the live site. It is not legal advice. Before you take real orders, have a Polish radca prawny or adwokat read this audit and the documents in this folder, and have your accountant check the tax items. The law is quoted from the ISAP consolidated texts and EUR-Lex pages listed in `OPEN-ITEMS.md`, section 8; where I could not confirm something in a primary source I say so.

Abbreviations: UPK = ustawa o prawach konsumenta (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1244); KC = Kodeks cywilny (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 795); PKE = Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1221); UPNPR = ustawa o przeciwdziałaniu nieuczciwym praktykom rynkowym (Dz.U. 2023 poz. 845); GPSR = Regulation (EU) 2023/988; RODO/GDPR = Regulation (EU) 2016/679; DSA = Regulation (EU) 2022/2065.

## 1. Executive summary

**State of the shop.** The checkout is technically open: it takes card payments with a **live** Stripe key and 4 products are published. I did not place an order. None of the legal pages exists on the live site (`/regulamin/`, `/polityka-prywatnosci/`, `/kontakt/` return 404), the WooCommerce "Terms" and "Privacy" page settings are empty, and the footer links only to the withdrawal page.

**Top problems (details in section 3):**

1. **No Regulamin, privacy policy or cookie policy is published and checkout links to none of them** (C-1). The mandatory pre-contract information is missing or not given on a durable medium (C-4). Consequences include a withdrawal period extended up to 12 months and no return costs for the consumer (art. 29 ust. 1, art. 23, art. 34 ust. 2 UPK).
2. **Google tracking runs before consent and after the visitor clicks "Odrzuć wszystkie"** (C-2). I observed GA4 and Google Ads requests on first load and `_ga` cookies after rejecting. The banner says the opposite.
3. **No product-safety information on product pages** and no evidence that electrical goods and toys are compliant (C-3). A missing manufacturer, identifier or warning online is fined up to PLN 500,000 (art. 76 of the Polish GPSR Act).
4. **The privacy notice does not match reality** (C-5): the old template said "no analytics, no transfers outside the EEA, Order Attribution off". The live site uses Google, Stripe, Hostinger, WooCommerce Order Attribution, an abandoned-cart plugin and dropshipping suppliers.
5. **Express wallet buttons** (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link) on product and checkout pages are not labelled "zamówienie z obowiązkiem zapłaty"; if the label is deficient, art. 17 ust. 4 UPK says **no contract is concluded** (H-2).
6. **Unsupported or placeholder claims** on the home page: "Gwarancja producenta na każdy produkt", "Łatwe zwroty / 14 dni", "Szybka dostawa" (H-3), a "Marki" strip with invented brand names and "Oferta tygodnia" badges on products that are not discounted (H-10).
7. **Crossed-out regular prices** on 24 of 28 catalogue products will be an Omnibus and misleading-price problem unless they are real earlier prices (H-6).
8. **WEEE / BDO / batteries / packaging** duties for electrical goods in a dropshipping chain are unresolved (H-8).
9. **Dropshipping without a supplier agreement**: promises on delivery, neutral packaging, returns, CE documents and refunds cannot be enforced (H-7).
10. **Reviews, abandoned-cart and e-mail tools** collect or send data without a consent or notice (H-5, H-9).

**What I wrote in this folder.** A complete, updated set of Polish documents (Regulamin, privacy policy, cookie policy, returns, complaints, delivery and payment, company data, product-safety block) and two templates (supplier agreement, data-processing agreement); an updated `OPEN-ITEMS.md` with a numbered checklist for you.

**Recommendation.** Pause orders (D13 in OPEN-ITEMS) until C-1 to C-5 and H-1, H-2 are fixed. Then have the lawyer review.
